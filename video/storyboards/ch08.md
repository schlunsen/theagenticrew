**Narrator:** AI voice (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD, English_magnetic_voiced_man).
**Logline:** Every agent session starts with amnesia and throws its lessons away. Active memory, a ship's log for agents, captures what was learned, files it, and serves it to every later session and every agent on the team.
**Inks:** the series set (federal, orange, yellow). Overprints: a halftone sea (federal over yellow), the shoal (orange over navy), yellow page highlights, the poisoned note (orange + federal).
**Motif:** the logbook: a brown book with paper pages. It is missing at the start, becomes the memory store, and sails on at the end while the crew changes.
