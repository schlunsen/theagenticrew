# App A · Agents as Pentesters: storyboard

**Narrator:** AI voice (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD, English_magnetic_voiced_man).
**Logline:** Continuous, automated security scanning as a case study in agentic engineering: a multi-phase pipeline, a sentinel that routes by change, absolute guardrails, a feedback loop, deduplication, tiered models and a human-in-the-loop dashboard.
**Inks:** federal + orange + yellow (series). Orange stands for the "hostile" agent; overprints: orange guardrail bars over navy, the yellow verify-glow over the dashboard.
**Tone note:** defensive, authorized testing only; guardrails and human oversight are the emphasis. Abstract diagrams, nothing operational.
