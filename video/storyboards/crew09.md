# crew09 · Hidden Instructions: storyboard

**Narrator:** English_ConfidentWoman (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD). 164.2 s.
**Logline:** Clawd reads the Navigator's post every morning, until a stranger's letter carries a paragraph written in white ink, meant only for him. The Navigator learns what makes that dangerous (three things meeting in one job, the legs of a stool) and how to kick a leg away.

## The idea

One recurring set: **the Navigator's harbour office**. It has a door with a brass mail slot, a round porthole onto the sea, a desk where Clawd sits and reads, a filing cabinet, a waste bin and a sea chart pinned on the wall. The film's single visual idea is **rose ink**: everything a stranger wrote *for the agent* is painted in rose. The Navigator sees only cream paper, while Clawd sees glowing rose lines of pictograms (an invoice, a bank, a paper plane, a bin, a "shh"). Rose ink drips into a glass of water (the injection), floods into Clawd's lunchbox mouth alongside the Navigator's own teal order card, slips under his hard hat, and turns into puppet strings at the end. The **lethal trifecta** is painted on the wall chart as three watercolour circles: ochre (private things, a strongbox), rose (strangers' text, a sealed envelope) and sea (a way out, a porthole with a paper plane). Where all three overlap, a **three-legged stool** rises, one leg per colour, carrying a pirate flag. Pull any one leg or slide any one circle away and the stool falls over. The ending rhymes with the opening: the same desk and the same rose letter, but this time the Navigator looks at it through her spyglass and sees the ink.

Colour arc: warm morning cream → rose creeping into everything → the painted chart → calm teal and ochre with the rose shut in the bin.
