# Crew 04 · What Is an Agent, Really?: storyboard

**Narrator:** AI voice (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD, English_ConfidentWoman). 2:26.
**Palette:** CREW sea-teal / ochre / cream, rose for "wrong", sap for "right". Colour arc: morning cream (the kitchen) → warm chart room → cool open sea (the wrong direction) → a night that passes → golden evening at the helm.

## The idea

**The loop is a ship's wheel.** In the chart room stands a big wooden helm wheel that is also a tiny round world: four stations stand on its rim like trees on a little planet (a spyglass on a tripod = observe, a chart on an easel = plan, a lever = act, a big lens = check). Clawd walks on top of the rim and the wheel turns under him, bringing each station up in turn, so "going round the loop" is something you can *see*. The wheel comes back at the end on the ship's deck: the Navigator takes its handles and steers, while Clawd keeps walking the loop on its rim. Steering, not typing. Recurring sets: the Navigator's kitchen table (tiny agents), the chart room with the wheel, the harbour office (keys), the quay (not a person / the spectrum / the engine and the car), the deck at sunset. Tiny Clawds from the hook come back in the loop shot as "a simple agent" and in the spectrum as autocomplete.
