# Ch 11 · Articulating Intent: storyboard

**Narrator:** AI voice (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD, English_magnetic_voiced_man).
**Logline:** Agents don't ask clarifying questions; they guess. A good prompt is a small spec: what, why, how to verify, what not to touch, split into checkable steps, planned before it's built.
**Inks:** Federal Blue + Orange + Yellow (series set). Featured overprints: the prompt card assembling (gold bars over paper), the guardrails over the bridge, the diff scroll (orange over federal), the speech ribbon.
**Motif:** the prompt card. It starts as a scrap with a bug on it and grows into a spec, a plan, and finally a saved skill in a folder.
