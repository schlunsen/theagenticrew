**Narrator:** AI voice (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD, English_magnetic_voiced_man).
**Logline:** Agents move fast, so Git becomes the backbone: small commits to undo, agent-written messages as documentation, a branch and a worktree per task, and the diff as the review screen.
**Inks:** Federal Blue + Orange + Yellow (series set). Featured overprints: orange over federal (a broken commit, a stray file), green (federal + yellow) for good commits, federal tone over yellow for the glass dome and the worktree folders.
**Motif:** the commit line, a navy rail with gold dots. It is the backbone in A, the path in B, forks in D and E, and scrolls as the log in H.
