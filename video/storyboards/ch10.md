# Ch 10 · The Agent Attack Surface: storyboard

**Narrator:** AI voice (MiniMax Speech 2.6 HD, English_magnetic_voiced_man).
**Logline:** To a model, data and instructions are the same tokens, so anyone who can put text in front of your agent can steer it. Injection is unsolved; design so a tricked agent can do little harm.
**Inks:** Federal Blue + Orange + Yellow (series set). Featured overprints: the hidden paragraph (yellow over the issue card), the lethal-trifecta Venn (three circles overprinting to a dark centre), leaking data packets (gold over federal), the sandbox glass.
**Motif:** the hooded stranger (a navy silhouette) who never touches the system, only its text; the keys, which the Skipper keeps at the end.
