# handson00 — The Hands-On Guide (watercolour overview, Hands-On Guide)

## The idea

The whole film happens in one **night-time drafting room painted as a blueprint**: the back wall is a sheet of deep
blueprint blue ruled with cyan grid, a slanted drafting table stands in the middle with a thick book on it, a pegboard
workbench on the left, a tall window on the right onto a dark harbour of lit windows, and an **amber terminal** that
glows like a work lamp. Pinned high on the wall is the cover's motif, **a ship's wheel drawn to dimension, with ten
handles** — one per exercise. Every time the learner finishes an exercise, one handle stops being a cyan line and
fills with real amber paint. That is the film's one visual idea: **the drawing becomes real**. Things arrive as cyan
blueprint outlines and flood with paint when they're done (the tools on the pegboard, the server, the shop), and in
the last shot the whole wheel fills in, drops off the wall onto a pedestal and the Navigator takes the helm. She starts
as a curious passenger reading a book (the book gets snatched away: this book is done, not read) and ends as captain
with a cap on her head; Clawd is her pair all the way through. Colour arc: cold blueprint blue with only the terminal
lit → more and more amber paint in the room → the wheel and the whole room warm at the end.

Words on screen: only "10" (the dimension callout by the wheel, on "Ten of them"). The ident and end card carry the
lettering. Palette: blueprint night #081a33 / #0f2a4f / #1a3d6e, line cyan #9fd3f5, chalk #eef4fa, safety amber
#f2a33a / #ffc56e, test-green and fail-rose as small accents.
