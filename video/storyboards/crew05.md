# crew05 — How to Give Good Instructions (watercolour, Crew Member's Guide)

## The idea

Every instruction in this film is a **paper slip** you hand to Clawd, and Clawd paints whatever the slip says onto a **big easel board** in a harbour-office chart room. The chart room is the recurring set: it is long enough to hold **two desks back to back**, Alex's (left, cooler, cluttered) and Maya's (right, warm, tidy), and the camera travels between them. Alex's slip is a single scribble; Maya's is a column of neat lines. The same Clawd trots from one desk to the other, so "same agent, same goal" is literally one character crossing one room. The middle of the film leaves the office for the harbour outside (the wall chart, the channel with buoys, the gangway of levels) and comes back to the office for the plan and the follow-up, ending on deck with the good slip in Clawd's claw as the ship sets out: colour moves from Alex's grey-teal gloom to Maya's warm ochre light.

Cast: **Maya** = the Navigator (teal sweater, ochre scarf). **Alex** = a second captain drawn in-scene (the Navigator's build re-coloured: ginger hair, rose sweater, slate scarf, a flat cap), always on the left.
Words on screen: only "PLAN" (the plan sheet's heading). The ident and end card carry the chapter lettering.
